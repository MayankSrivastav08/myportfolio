About Me

Who Am I?

I am a passionate software developer with a keen interest in building innovative solutions. With a strong foundation in programming and problem-solving, I strive to create impactful applications that enhance user experiences. My journey in the tech world is driven by curiosity and a desire to continuously learn and grow.


I’m a B.Sc. Computer Science student currently in my second year, building my foundation toward a career in Software Development and Software Engineering. Right now, I’m focused on strengthening my programming and computer science fundamentals through C++, Data Structures & Algorithms, SQL, DBMS, and regular problem solving on LeetCode. Alongside DSA, I’m learning Full-Stack Development to understand how different parts of a web application work together and to turn what I learn into practical projects. I enjoy learning by building, solving problems, understanding why things work, and improving through consistent practice. My goal is to gradually move from learning concepts to creating real-world software projects that solve practical problems. In the long term, I want to grow into a strong software engineer and eventually build software products of my own. I’m still early in the journey, but I’m focused on learning consistently, staying curious, and improving one step at a time. 🤝 Always open to connecting with developers, mentors, and fellow learners in Computer Science and Software Development.
